---
title: "Atlassian Rovo"
description: "Let Rubix read Jira tickets and Confluence pages while it investigates."
---

Connect Atlassian Rovo so Rubix can read Jira tickets and Confluence pages. It's how Rubix finds a related ticket or a runbook in your Atlassian site. To open incidents from Jira, set up [Jira](/integrations/jira) as an alert source too.

## Connect it

<Steps titleSize="h3">
  <Step title="Allow RubixKube in Atlassian">
    An Atlassian admin adds `https://console.rubixkube.ai/**` under **Atlassian Administration > Rovo > Rovo MCP server > Domains**. You only need this for sign-in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect it in RubixKube">
    Go to **Customize > Integrations** and pick **Atlassian Rovo**. Choose **Sign in with Atlassian**, or paste a **Service account key**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Rubix can read

The **Reads** section shows one row for Jira tickets and one for Confluence docs, for each Atlassian site the connection reaches. See [the Reads check](/integrations/overview#see-what-a-connection-can-read).

Anything that changes Jira or Confluence follows your [modes](/using/modes), and you can turn it off with its switch in the **Tools** list.
