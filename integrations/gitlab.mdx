Connect GitLab so Rubix can see what shipped before something broke, and cite it in the RCA.

## Connect it

<Steps titleSize="h3">
  <Step title="Open GitLab in RubixKube">
    Go to **Customize > Integrations** and pick **GitLab**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Sign in to GitLab in the new tab and allow RubixKube. The page shows GitLab as connected on its own.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For a self-managed GitLab, add it as a [custom MCP server](/integrations/custom-mcp-servers) at `https://<your-host>/api/v4/mcp`, using your own OAuth application. Your server needs a public `https` address.

## What Rubix can read

Rubix reads what the person who signed in can see. The connection's tools come from GitLab's own server, and the **Tools** list shows each one. Anything that changes GitLab follows your [modes](/using/modes), and you can turn it off with its switch.

Press **Test** to run [the Reads check](/integrations/overview#see-what-a-connection-can-read).
