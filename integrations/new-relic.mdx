Connect New Relic so Rubix can query what New Relic holds about a service while it works out why something broke.

## Connect it

<Steps titleSize="h3">
  <Step title="Open New Relic in RubixKube">
    Go to **Customize > Integrations**, pick **New Relic** and choose your region: US, EU or Japan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Choose **Sign in with New Relic**, or paste a **User API key**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Rubix can read

The connection's tools come from New Relic's own server, and the **Tools** list shows each one. Press **Test** to run [the Reads check](/integrations/overview#see-what-a-connection-can-read).

New Relic isn't an alert source. To send its alerts to RubixKube, point a New Relic webhook at [your own webhook](/integrations/webhooks).
