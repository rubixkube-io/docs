---
title: "Notion"
description: "Let Rubix read your runbooks and team docs in Notion."
---

Connect Notion so Rubix can read your runbooks, service notes and past post-mortems while it investigates and when you ask it something in chat.

## Connect it

<Steps titleSize="h3">
  <Step title="Open Notion in RubixKube">
    Go to **Customize > Integrations** and pick **Notion**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Sign in to Notion in the new tab and allow RubixKube. The page shows Notion as connected on its own.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Rubix can read

Rubix reads what Notion gives this connection. If it can't find a page, check that page's access in Notion.

The connection's tools come from Notion's own server, and the **Tools** list shows each one. Anything that changes Notion follows your [modes](/using/modes), and you can turn it off with its switch. To teach Rubix where your runbooks live, write a [skill](/using/skills).
