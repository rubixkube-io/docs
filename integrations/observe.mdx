Connect Observe so a new alarm opens an incident in RubixKube, and the alarm ending resolves it. You can also connect Observe as a tool Rubix reads while it investigates.

## When you'd use this

- **Your logs and monitors live in Observe.** Each alarm gets an investigation without anyone opening a worksheet.
- **You want RCAs built on your own data.** With a connection, Rubix reads your Observe datasets as evidence.

## Connect it

<Steps titleSize="h3">
  <Step title="Make its webhook in RubixKube">
    Go to **Environments > Connect** and pick **Observe**. Give it a name, then choose **Make its webhook**. Copy the URL. It's shown once, and anyone who has it can post to it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a shared action in Observe">
    In Observe, go to **Monitors > View shared actions > New shared action** and choose **Webhook**. Paste the URL.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the payload">
    Paste this as the action's payload. The Connect dialog shows the same text, with a copy button.

```json
{
  "event": "{{alert.type.eventType}}",
  "alert_id": "{{{alert.id}}}",
  "monitor_id": "{{monitor.id}}",
  "monitor": "{{monitor.name}}",
  "severity": "{{alert.severity.level}}",
  "title": "{{alert.severity.level}}: {{monitor.name}}",
  "description": "{{monitor.description}}",
  "started_at": "{{alert.start.rfc3339}}",
  "ended_at": "{{alert.end.rfc3339}}",
  "url": "{{{alert.url}}}",
  "data_url": "{{{alert.dataUrl}}}",
  "values": [{{#alert.values}}{"name": "{{name}}", "value": "{{value}}"}{{^isLast}},{{/isLast}}{{/alert.values}}],
  "tags": [{{#alert.tags}}{"name": "{{name}}", "value": "{{value}}"}{{^isLast}},{{/isLast}}{{/alert.tags}}],
  "resources": [{{#alert.resources}}{"label": "{{label}}", "name": "{{value}}", "url": "{{{url}}}"}{{^isLast}},{{/isLast}}{{/alert.resources}}]
}
```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach it to your monitors">
    Add the shared action to each monitor you want, and turn on end notifications. Without them, RubixKube never hears that the alarm ended.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What opens an incident and what resolves it

| Observe event | What happens |
|---|---|
| `NewAlarm` | Opens the incident |
| `AlarmConditionEnded` | Resolves the incident |
| Anything else | Ignored |

**Severity** comes from the alarm: Critical is critical, Error is high, Warning is medium, and anything else is low.

Each alarm is one incident.

## What you'll see

The source appears on **Environments** as soon as you make its webhook, then shows as connected when the first message arrives. Its page has an **Events** tab with the last 20 messages, so you can check what Observe really sent.

The automation **Observe alerts become incidents** is on under **Automations**. If events arrive and no incident opens, the source page says so and offers **Turn on**.

Each incident carries the alert's details and a link back to Observe. Triage, the RCA and Tasks then work as they do for any other incident.

## What Rubix can read too

To let Rubix read Observe while it investigates, open **Customize > Integrations**, pick **Observe**, and enter your customer id and region. Your customer id is the number at the start of your Observe address: for `https://123456789012.observeinc.com`, it's `123456789012`. Then choose **Sign in with Observe** or paste an **API token**.

The connection's **Reads** section shows what Rubix can reach: logs from each dataset, monitors and Observe's docs. See [the Reads check](/integrations/overview#see-what-a-connection-can-read).
