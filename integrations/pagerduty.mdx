---
title: "PagerDuty"
description: "Turn PagerDuty incidents into RubixKube incidents, and let Rubix read PagerDuty while it investigates."
---

Connect PagerDuty so an incident there opens one in RubixKube, and resolving it there resolves it here. Rubix then works out the cause while your on-call process carries on as usual.

This goes one way, from PagerDuty into RubixKube. Nothing triggers, acknowledges or resolves anything in PagerDuty.

## When you'd use this

- **PagerDuty is how your team hears about problems.** Keep it. RubixKube adds the investigation behind each one.
- **You want the cause next to the alert.** The person who picks up the incident can read what Rubix found so far.
- **You use several tools.** PagerDuty incidents sit next to what observers and other tools report, and related ones group together.

## Connect it

<Steps titleSize="h3">
  <Step title="Make its webhook in RubixKube">
    Go to **Environments > Connect** and pick **PagerDuty**. Give it a name, then choose **Make its webhook**. Copy the URL. It's shown once, and anyone who has it can post to it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the webhook in PagerDuty">
    In PagerDuty, go to **Integrations > Generic Webhooks (v3)** and add a new webhook. Paste the URL.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the events">
    Choose whether it covers your whole account, a team or one service. Subscribe to `incident.triggered`, `incident.reopened` and `incident.resolved`. RubixKube ignores other events.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What opens an incident and what resolves it

| PagerDuty event | What happens |
|---|---|
| `incident.triggered`, `incident.reopened` | Opens the incident |
| `incident.resolved` | Resolves the incident |
| Anything else, such as `incident.acknowledged` | Ignored |

**Severity** comes from the priority first: P1 is critical and P2 is high. Without those, high urgency is high, low urgency is low, and anything else is medium.

Each PagerDuty incident is one RubixKube incident. Rubix's RCA stays in RubixKube.

## What you'll see

The source appears on **Environments** as soon as you make its webhook, then shows as connected when the first message arrives. Its page has an **Events** tab with the last 20 messages, so you can check what PagerDuty really sent.

The automation **PagerDuty incidents become incidents** is on under **Automations**. If events arrive and no incident opens, the source page says so and offers **Turn on**.

Each incident carries the alert's details and a link back to PagerDuty. Triage, the RCA and Tasks then work as they do for any other incident.

## What Rubix can read too

To let Rubix read PagerDuty while it investigates, open **Customize > Integrations**, pick **PagerDuty**, choose your region (US or EU) and paste a **User API key**.

The connection's **Reads** section then shows what Rubix can reach: change events and incidents. See [the Reads check](/integrations/overview#see-what-a-connection-can-read).
