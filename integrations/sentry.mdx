---
title: "Sentry"
description: "Turn Sentry issues and alerts into incidents, and let Rubix read Sentry while it investigates."
---

Connect Sentry so a new issue opens an incident in RubixKube, and resolving it in Sentry resolves it here. You can also connect Sentry as a tool Rubix reads while it investigates.

## When you'd use this

- **The problem is in your code, not your infrastructure.** An observer sees a healthy pod. Sentry sees the exception.
- **A new error appeared after a release.** Rubix can look at what shipped and what else changed.
- **You want errors and infrastructure in one place.** A Sentry issue and a crash-looping pod can land in the same group.

## Connect it

<Steps titleSize="h3">
  <Step title="Make its webhook in RubixKube">
    Go to **Environments > Connect** and pick **Sentry**. Give it a name, then choose **Make its webhook**. Copy the URL. It's shown once, and anyone who has it can post to it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create an internal integration in Sentry">
    In Sentry, go to **Settings > Developer Settings > Custom Integrations** and create a new **Internal Integration**. Paste the URL as its webhook URL.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set its permissions and webhooks">
    Give it **Read** access to **Issue & Event**, turn on **Alert Rule Action**, and tick the **issue** webhook. Save it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it to your alert rules">
    To send alerts as well as new issues, add the integration as an action on the Sentry alert rules you want.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What opens an incident and what resolves it

| Sentry event | What happens |
|---|---|
| Issue created or unresolved | Opens the incident |
| Issue resolved or archived | Resolves the incident |
| Alert rule fires | Opens the incident for that issue |
| Metric alert critical or warning | Opens the incident |
| Metric alert resolved | Resolves the incident |

**Severity** for an issue comes from its level: `fatal` is critical, `error` is high, `warning` is medium, and anything else is low. A metric alert is critical when it's critical and medium when it's a warning.

Each Sentry issue is one incident, and each metric alert is one incident.

## What you'll see

The source appears on **Environments** as soon as you make its webhook, then shows as connected when the first message arrives. Its page has an **Events** tab with the last 20 messages, so you can check what Sentry really sent.

The automation **Sentry issues become incidents** is on under **Automations**. If events arrive and no incident opens, the source page says so and offers **Turn on**.

Each incident carries the alert's details and a link back to Sentry. Triage, the RCA and Tasks then work as they do for any other incident. For an alert rule, the incident includes the exception and the last frames of the stack trace.

## What Rubix can read too

To let Rubix read Sentry while it investigates, open **Customize > Integrations**, pick **Sentry**, and choose **Sign in with Sentry** or paste a **User auth token**.

The connection's **Reads** section shows what Rubix can reach. See [the Reads check](/integrations/overview#see-what-a-connection-can-read).
