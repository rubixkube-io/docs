## The basics

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is RubixKube?">
    RubixKube helps teams run infrastructure with an agent. It turns what breaks into incidents, investigates each one into an RCA, and suggests Tasks. Rubix is the agent you work with. See [What RubixKube does](/getting-started/overview).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What can I connect?">
    Observers for Kubernetes, AWS, Google Cloud and servers or VMs running Linux or macOS. Also Datadog, PagerDuty, Grafana, Sentry, Jira, Linear and Observe by webhook, and your own webhooks. See [Connect a source](/getting-started/connect-your-environment).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How long does it take to see something?">
    An observer usually reports in within a minute of the install. A tool's source appears on its first message. Incidents appear when something breaks.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What's the difference between a source and an environment?">
    A source is a cluster, cloud account, machine or tool. An environment, such as staging, is a label you put across sources.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Microsoft Teams work?">
    Not yet. It shows as **Coming soon** in **Customize > Channels**. Slack works today on Business and Enterprise.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Incidents and RCAs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why hasn't my RCA started?">
    There's a wait by severity: none for critical, 2 minutes for high, 5 for medium and 10 for low. Observers also hold an incident to confirm it, unless it's critical. The incident page says when the RCA starts. See [Troubleshooting](/support/troubleshooting#the-rca-hasnt-started).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I trust an RCA?">
    Check it. Rubix can be wrong, so every RCA shows its evidence and its confidence. It says **Root cause**, **Likely cause** or **Not determined**. When it can't tell, it says so. Answer **Was this the cause?** so your team knows which ones held up.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does every incident get an RCA?">
    No. Incidents in a group share one RCA. Incidents that recover before the RCA starts don't get one, and neither do alerts triage marks as known. When no RCA is coming, you can start one with **Run RCA again**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does Needs you mean?">
    The incident recovered, and RubixKube is waiting for someone to say what fixed it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do incidents close on their own?">
    Only if you turn it on in **Settings > Incidents**. Otherwise they stay open until someone closes them. If a problem comes back later, RubixKube opens a new incident linked to the old one.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Rubix and changes

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will Rubix change things without asking?">
    That depends on the mode. Observe changes nothing. Assist asks before each change. Auto changes things without asking, and destructive changes always ask. New organizations start in Observe, with Assist as the highest mode until an admin changes it. See [Modes](/using/modes).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do RCAs change anything?">
    No. RCAs only read. Rubix suggests Tasks, and people decide what to do with them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Rubix apply the Fix on its own?">
    A Fix is a Task, a piece of work with an owner. To have Rubix carry it out, ask it in chat, and the mode decides what happens next.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I add my own rules?">
    Yes. Hooks run your own checks before Rubix changes something or reads a message, like a change freeze. They come with Business and Enterprise. See [Customize](/using/customize).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Your data

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What does RubixKube store?">
    Incidents, RCAs, Tasks and comments, plus the evidence behind them. Evidence can include events, log lines and what a tool's alert sent. Observers also send metadata and metrics, and tools send the webhook messages you point at RubixKube.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What can the Kubernetes observer read?">
    Its role is in the manifest you apply, so you can read every line first. [Connect a source](/getting-started/connect-your-environment#what-an-observer-needs) has a summary.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do people sign in?">
    With email, Google or GitHub. For anything else, write to connect@rubixkube.ai.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How long do you keep my data, and where?">
    Write to connect@rubixkube.ai and we'll tell you what applies to your plan.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Plans

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What are the plans?">
    Individual has 1 environment, 10 investigations a month and 1 user. Business has 3 environments, 100 investigations and 5 users. Enterprise is set for your organization. See [pricing](https://rubixkube.ai/pricing).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What needs a paid plan?">
    Slack, Automations, Hooks, Webhooks, your own models, custom integrations, more than one environment, and inviting Members. Everything else is on Individual too.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do I see my usage?">
    In **Settings > Usage and plan**, along with your AI token usage.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
