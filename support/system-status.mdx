---
title: "System status"
description: "Check whether RubixKube itself is having a problem, and what to do during an outage."
---

When something feels slow or wrong, check [rubixkube.ai/status](https://rubixkube.ai/status) first. It tells you whether the problem is on our side or yours.

## What the status page shows

The banner at the top gives the overall state: **All systems operational**, **Some systems degraded** or **Service disruption detected**. Below it, services are listed under Platform, Intelligence, Data and Infrastructure. Each one shows **Operational**, **Degraded** or **Outage**, with a bar of recent checks and its uptime over 7 days. The page refreshes on its own.

## If something looks wrong

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the status page">
    If the banner shows a problem, it's on our side. You don't need to change anything.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your source">
    If the status page is clear, open **Environments** and look at the source. A problem in your own infrastructure shows up there and in your incidents. It doesn't appear on the status page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use Troubleshooting">
    The [Troubleshooting guide](/support/troubleshooting) lists what the console says and what to check.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write to us">
    Email connect@rubixkube.ai with your Organization ID, the time you saw it, and any screenshots. See [what to include](/support/troubleshooting#before-you-write-to-us).
  </Step>
</Steps>
