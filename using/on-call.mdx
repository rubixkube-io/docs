On-Call is a standalone RubixKube product for receiving alerts, organizing coverage, and coordinating human response. Use it from the **On-Call** area in the current RubixKube Console. A service owns an escalation policy; the policy can target responders directly or resolve a schedule when a page is due. Incidents, pages, and handoffs stay visible alongside the shared Incidents area.

On-Call is enabled separately for each organization; a RubixKube superadmin may need to enable access if it is missing from the product navigation. No Kubernetes cluster, Observer, or AI entitlement is required to receive alerts or page responders. On-Call AI is a separate, default-off entitlement; enabling On-Call does not turn on AI investigation or triage. See [capabilities and availability](/using/on-call-capabilities) for provider limits.

## Set up a service

1. Open **On-Call → Teams** and create the team that owns the service. Add responders and choose their roles.
2. Open **On-Call → Schedules** and create a staffed rotation. Choose its timezone and handoff time, review the coverage preview, and add overrides for planned absences.
3. Open **On-Call → Policies** and create ordered escalation steps. Each step can target a person or schedule and has a delay before the next step. Save the policy and use its preview to check who would be paged now.
4. Open **On-Call → Services**, create the service, and select its team and escalation policy. Configure routing, urgency and maintenance rules on the service page.
5. In the service's **Integrations** tab, add Alertmanager or a generic webhook. Save the integration key when it is shown: it cannot be retrieved later. Use **Test** to send a sample alert through the ingest path and verify the resulting incident.

## Start with Slack notifications

Connect the organization Slack integration and link your responder identity with `/oncall connect`. Then open **On-Call → My On-Call → Notifications** and put Slack first for the urgency levels you cover. Add a later email rule as a fallback where email relay is configured. Rules and delays are per responder, so confirm your test page in the delivery ledger before relying on a route. See [Slack setup and commands](/integrations/slack).

Only channels configured for your organization and account are available. Email depends on relay setup. SMS and voice are not active in this release; provider configuration and acceptance remain open. The delivery ledger shows what Pager attempted and why a channel was skipped.

## Pair the mobile app

In **Apps → On-Call mobile**, generate a short-lived pairing code. Enter it in the RubixKube On-Call app while signed in as the same person. The code can be used once and expires; generate a new one if it expires. The Apps page lists paired sessions and lets you revoke one if a phone is lost or replaced.

The app provides a compact view of your pages and the current roster, plus incident acknowledgement, resolution, escalation, manual paging, and timed coverage overrides. Push delivery depends on platform provider setup and device permission; pairing alone does not guarantee a push notification. APNs and critical iOS alert delivery have not passed acceptance. SMS and voice through Telnyx are not available yet.

## Work an incident

The shared **Incidents** list filters by status, urgency, service, team and ownership. Open an incident to see its alerts, timeline, assigned responders, escalation steps and notification ledger. Acknowledge to take responsibility, add an operational note, reassign or escalate when another responder is needed, and resolve when the issue is addressed. Resolving also resolves the incident's open alerts.

**My On-Call** shows your current and next coverage, assigned incidents and a test-page entry point. **Now** shows current level-zero coverage for active services; `?kiosk=1` gives the board a full-screen view.

## Swaps and handoffs

Request a swap from a future shift on its schedule page. The proposed recipient can accept or decline before the shift begins. A schedule handoff asks the outgoing responder for an optional note shortly before the boundary, then shows a summary to the incoming responder. Review and acknowledge a delivered handoff from the schedule or **My On-Call**.

## Operational checks

- If an alert creates no incident, inspect the service integration, routing rules, maintenance windows and event rules. The generic webhook mapping preview can identify a missing title or dedup key before sending.
- If a page is skipped, inspect the incident's **Notifications** panel and the responder's notification settings. A verified phone or linked chat identity may be required.
- If coverage is empty, inspect the schedule preview, overrides and escalation policy targets. The **Now** board shows the resulting coverage.

On-Call pages are separate from [general RubixKube notifications](/using/notifications), which distribute insights, reports and action updates.
